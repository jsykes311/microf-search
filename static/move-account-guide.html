Moving a dealer ID to another account

Use this when the same dealer has two accounts in ActiveCampaign (for example one for the Microf dealer ID and one for the Optimus dealer ID) and you want them on one account, or when a dealer ID was filed under the wrong account.

Before you start

  • A dealer ID (DID) is an SLP record. Moving a dealer ID means moving its SLP onto the other account. Its contacts and deals are separate and move only if you tick them.
  • Decide which account you are keeping. The source is the account you are emptying. The target is the account that stays. The source is the only one that can be deleted.
Which account should I keep? Keep the one that is more complete: usually the older activation, more contacts, a filled-in DBA name and revenue, and any Strategic Partner field. If an account is only a stub (one contact, few fields), make that one the source.

  • SLPs are the dealer IDs. Leave ticked to move them.
  • Contacts keep their job titles. Contacts marked already on target are not added twice; they are simply taken off the source.
  • Deals move with the account. If you plan to delete the source, leave them ticked so they are not lost.
  • Untick anything you want to leave behind.

Moving just one dealer ID

To move one dealer ID without touching the rest of the account:

  1. Choose the account it is on now as the source, and where it should go as the target.
  2. In Step 3, tick only that dealer ID's SLP. Untick every contact and deal.
  3. Leave Delete source account unticked.
  4. Click Move Selected Items.

The dealer ID now sits on the target, and the source account is unchanged.

After the move

  • Open the target in ActiveCampaign and confirm the SLPs, contacts and deals you expected are there.
  • Account fields are not copied. Address, revenue, DBA name and Oracle IDs on the account itself stay as they are on the target. If the source had better values, copy them over by hand before you delete it. They are in the backup.
  • Duplicate people are not merged. If the same person was on both accounts under different email addresses, you will now see them twice. Combine them in ActiveCampaign if needed.
